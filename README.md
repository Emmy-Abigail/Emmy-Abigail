### About Me

I'm a Computer Engineering student at Universidad Peruana Cayetano Heredia (UPCH), interested in building software solutions that bring together different areas of technology to solve concrete problems.

My training and projects have focused mainly on **software engineering**, and I'm currently deepening my knowledge of **Machine Learning and data science**. I like to understand the problem first and then design the solution: from architecture and databases to application development, and exploring Machine Learning models when they can add real value.

I aim to build systems that don't just work, but are well structured, maintainable, and built with a clear purpose.
